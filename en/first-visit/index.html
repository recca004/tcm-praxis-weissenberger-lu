First Appointment

Your First Visit: What to Expect

Step-by-step guidance to ensure your first TCM consultation is comfortable, clear, and reassuring.

1

Scheduling & Arrival

Book your appointment online or by phone. Please arrive about 5–10 minutes early so you can settle in comfortably.

2

Medical Intake & Assessment

We spend 30–45 minutes exploring your health history, current complaints, and lifestyle. This includes tongue and pulse diagnostics.

3

Treatment & Planning

You receive your first targeted therapy (such as gentle acupuncture) and discuss the proposed follow-up schedule and recommendations.

What to Bring With You

  • Health insurance card: Required for administrative patient onboarding.
  • Recent medical reports: If relevant, bring medication lists, lab results, or imaging summaries.
  • Comfortable clothing: Loose pants and tops allow easy access to acupuncture points on arms and legs.

Helpful Tips Before Your Visit

  • Have a light meal: Do not arrive on an entirely empty stomach or immediately after a heavy banquet.
  • Avoid brushing the tongue: Please refrain from scraping your tongue coating or drinking coffee right before the appointment, as this alters the diagnostic tongue picture.
  • 24-hour cancellation rule: Please give at least 24 hours notice if you need to reschedule.

Book your initial session online or phone the clinic directly.
