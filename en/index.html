Swiss Health Insurance

Supplementary Coverage (VVG)

Basic health insurance (KVG) generally does not cover natural healthcare. However, most Swiss supplementary insurance plans (VVG) reimburse a substantial portion of TCM treatments.

Check your policy: Inquire with your health insurer beforehand regarding your annual complementary allowance.
Tariff 590: You will receive an official claim form (Tarif 590) to submit directly to your insurance company.
No GP referral needed: Most policies allow direct booking without a physician referral.

Supplementary Insurance & Coverage

Whether and to what extent your health insurer covers the costs depends on your supplementary plan, the treatment and your insurer’s conditions. Please confirm coverage directly with your insurer before your appointment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers before scheduling your first session.

We allow 75–90 minutes for the initial visit. We review your medical background, conduct tongue and pulse diagnostics, and perform your first treatment during the same appointment.

Acupuncture needles are hair-thin. You may feel a tiny momentary sensation or a subtle warm tingling called "De-Qi", which many patients find deeply relaxing.

With Swiss supplementary health insurance (VVG), most insurers cover 70–90% of the cost. We recommend contacting your insurer prior to your visit to confirm exact coverage.

Please notify us at least 24 hours in advance if you cannot attend your appointment so that the time slot can be offered to another patient.
