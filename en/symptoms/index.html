Health Concerns

Common Health Concerns

Many people visit us with a specific concern. Here we introduce four common issues and explain how a TCM consultation approaches them.

01

Back Pain

Tension in the neck and shoulders, a dull ache in the lower back, or recurring pain after long hours of sitting — back problems show up in many ways.

In a TCM consultation: We discuss the course of your pain, its triggers, and daily strain. Using tongue and pulse assessment, we place it within a TCM pattern and talk through whether acupuncture, Tuina, or cupping might suit you.

Book a consultation
02

Migraine

Recurring headache attacks — often with light sensitivity or nausea — can interrupt everyday life.

In a TCM consultation: We record how often attacks occur, what triggers them, and what accompanies them. From a TCM perspective, we look at the pattern behind the pain and discuss options such as acupuncture.

Book a consultation
03

Stress & Sleep

Ongoing tension, a racing mind at night, and restless sleep are closely linked in the TCM view.

In a TCM consultation: We talk through your daily load, sleep rhythm, and energy. From there we suggest an approach — calming acupuncture, for example, or supportive herbal prescriptions.

Book a consultation
04

Digestion

Bloating, a feeling of fullness, or irregular digestion can noticeably affect daily wellbeing.

In a TCM consultation: We review eating habits, daily rhythm, and stress together, then discuss whether Tuina, a herbal prescription, or acupuncture fits your concern.

Other Concerns Discussed in Acupuncture Consultations

Pain & Movement

  • Joint pain
  • Neck & spinal complaints
  • Musculoskeletal concerns
  • Sprains
  • Muscle & tendon injuries
  • Tendinitis
  • Bursitis

Sleep, Energy & Wellbeing

  • Sleep problems
  • Stress
  • Burnout
  • Daytime sleepiness
  • Fatigue & weakness
  • Difficulty concentrating
  • Mood-related concerns

Allergies & Breathing

  • Allergic conditions
  • Asthma (complementary — never a substitute for medical care)

Further Consultation Topics

  • Neurological concerns (medically assessed only)
  • Psychosomatic concerns
  • Smoking cessation (supportive)
  • Childbirth preparation (by arrangement only)

These topics are sometimes discussed during an acupuncture consultation. Suitability is assessed individually — acupuncture does not replace medical assessment or treatment.
