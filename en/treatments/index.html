How do we select your treatment plan?

In Traditional Chinese Medicine, there is no generic formula. Every patient is assessed individually through three complementary diagnostics:

  • Comprehensive Intake: In-depth discussion of sleep, digestion, stress, energy levels, and health history.
  • Tongue Examination: Color, shape, and coating provide insight into internal organ systems and fluid dynamics.
  • Radial Pulse Diagnosis: Assessing the quality, depth, and rhythm of 12 distinct pulse positions at both wrists.
