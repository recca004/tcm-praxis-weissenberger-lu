My Approach & Treatment Philosophy

Traditional Chinese Medicine has been my life's vocation. My conviction is that effective medical care requires taking time to listen attentively and understand each patient as a whole person.

In TCM, good health is not merely the absence of discomfort—it is the harmonious balance between bodily functions, emotional equilibrium, and lifestyle. By combining traditional diagnostics (tongue and pulse examination) with acupuncture, herbal prescriptions, and manual therapies like Tuina, I help your body awaken its self-regulating healing capacity.
