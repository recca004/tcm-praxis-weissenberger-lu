Health Insurance & Reimbursement

Whether and to what extent your health insurer covers TCM treatment depends on your supplementary insurance policy, the treatment method, the insurer’s recognition of the practitioner, and the insurer’s own rules and limits. Please confirm coverage with your insurer before your appointment.
