Swiss Supplementary Health Insurance (VVG)

In Switzerland, basic mandatory health insurance (KVG) does not cover naturopathic practitioners. However, if you hold supplementary insurance for complementary medicine (VVG), coverage depends on your policy, the treatment and your insurer’s recognition of the practitioner. Please confirm reimbursement directly with your insurer before your appointment.

  • Accreditations: Min Weissenberger-Lu holds the necessary Swiss recognitions (EMR / ASCA / SBO-TCM) [CONFIRM].
  • Reimbursement receipt: You will receive a standard Tariff 590 invoice with claim voucher to submit to your insurer.

Payment & Cancellation Terms

Simple, transparent administrative processes:

  • Payment methods: Bank transfer invoice, TWINT, or cash payment upon agreement [CONFIRM].
  • 24-hour notice: Please provide at least 24 hours notice for cancellations to avoid being billed for the reserved slot.
